About Us

Company

Xindadi was built in 1999. Our main products are gas grills, wood pellet grills, gas griddles, charcoal grills, metal tool cabinets, workbenches and miter saw stands.

During the past 25 years of high-speed development, we are already on the name list of Suzhou Government Top 100 Famous Export Enterprises, and we are authorised as a “High-Tech Industrial Enterprise” by the Jiangsu Provincial Government.

Our turnover has exceeded 40 million US dollars per year over the past 5 years, and we still keep an increasing rate of 35% per year, with the support of the Suzhou Government and all our customers from the worldwide.

About Us

Company

TOH (Cambodia) Industrial Co., Ltd. was built in 2018, located in Sihanoukville Special Economy Zone (SSEZ) in Cambodia. We are the biggest manufacturer of tool storage and saw stands in Cambodia. Our products are exported to the US market.

We have 1200 workers in peak season, and the factory size is about 960708 square feet. We welcome our customers from all over the world to visit our factory, develop products with our team, or have your product customized.

We guarantee the quality by our automatic machines, equipment and our reliable quality system. Well-equipped facilities and skilled workers enable us to serve our customers with competitive price, good quality and on-time delivery.
